# FS-002 Contact Management

## SmartAssist-First Principle

Contact Management is designed to be AI-assisted by default.

SmartAssist is available throughout the Contact Management experience.

SmartAssist may:

- Suggest existing contacts
- Suggest duplicate contacts
- Suggest contact updates
- Suggest company associations
- Surface relevant Personal Notes before meetings

SmartAssist assists users.

SmartAssist does not make decisions on behalf of users.

All AI-generated actions require user review and approval.

SmartAssist may surface insights and recommendations based on contacts, companies, opportunities, meetings, and historical interactions.


## Purpose

Create a single source of truth for people and contacts within SmartCRM.

The system must support known, partial, and unknown information while following the Reality First principle.

## Contact Fields

### Core Fields

Contact ID

- Phone Numbers (0..*)
- Preferred Contact Method (Optional)

- Status

## Contact Status

A contact may have one of the following statuses:

- Active
- Archived

## Reality First Principle

SmartCRM shall never invent contact information.

Unknown information shall remain unknown until verified.

Examples:

Allowed:
- Unknown
- Not Provided

Not Allowed:
- Fake names
- Assumed emails
- Invented phone numbers

AI suggestions require user approval.

# Contact Registry

## Contact Registry Principle

The Contact Registry is the single source of truth for Contact records across SmartCRM.

All platform capabilities shall derive Contact information from the Contact Registry.

Future SmartCRM capabilities shall reuse Contact data from the Contact Registry.

Independent Contact stores are prohibited.

Examples of prohibited patterns:

- Opportunity-specific Contact stores
- Project-specific Contact stores
- Meeting-specific Contact stores
- Workspace-specific Contact stores
- Intelligence-specific Contact stores

The Contact Registry remains authoritative for all Contact Facts.

# Contact Model

```ts
interface Contact {

  firstName?: string;

  lastName?: string;

  fullName?: string;

  jobTitle?: string;

  emails?: string[];

  phoneNumbers?: string[];

  preferredContactMethod?: string;

  linkedInUrl?: string;

  companyId?: string;

  ownerId?: string;

  status: ContactStatus;

  personalNotes?: string;

  createdAt: string;

  updatedAt: string;
}
```

## Contact Status Type

```ts
type ContactStatus =
  | "active"
  | "archived";
```

The platform shall support:

```text
Active
Archived
```

Status changes shall be auditable.

Archived Contacts shall remain available for reporting and historical analysis.

The Contact record remains the system of record for Contact information.

---

## Contact Facts Principle

Contact Facts represent verified information about a person.

Examples include:

- First Name
- Last Name
- Full Name
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Job Title
- Company Association
- LinkedIn URL
- Status

Contact Facts originate from the Contact Registry.

Contact Facts remain authoritative until modified by authorized users.

---

## Contact Intelligence Separation Principle

Contact Intelligence represents advisory information generated by SmartAssist.

Examples include:

- Duplicate Detection Suggestions
- Contact Enrichment Suggestions
- Meeting Preparation Suggestions
- Relationship Visibility Indicators
- Engagement Insights
- Contact Coverage Indicators

Contact Intelligence is advisory.

Contact Intelligence is not system-of-record data.

Contact Intelligence shall remain distinguishable from Contact Facts.

Contact Intelligence shall never automatically modify Contact records.

Unknown remains preferred over fiction.

The system shall allow authorized users to create Contact records.

- Contact creation shall be auditable.
- Contacts may be created with partial information.
- Unknown values shall be supported.
- SmartAssist shall not automatically create Contact records.

## FR-002 View Contact

- Company

- Primary Phone
- Owner

---

## FR-003 Update Contact

The system shall allow authorized users to modify Contact records.

Requirements:

- Changes shall be auditable.
- Historical data shall be preserved where appropriate.
- SmartAssist shall not automatically modify Contact records.

---

## FR-004 Contact Ownership

The system shall support Contact ownership.

Requirements:

- Ownership is optional.
- Ownership changes must be auditable.
- Ownership changes shall not automatically modify Company ownership.
- Ownership changes shall not automatically modify Opportunity ownership.

---

## FR-005 Contact Registry

The Contact Registry shall be the single source of truth for Contact information.

Requirements:

- Future capabilities shall reuse the Contact Registry.
- Duplicate Contact stores are prohibited.
- Platform capabilities shall reference Contact records rather than duplicate them.
