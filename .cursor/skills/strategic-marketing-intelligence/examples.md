# Strategic Marketing Intelligence — good vs bad

## 1. Vanity expo

**Facts:** 8,000 attendees, mixed public. Few plant owners in CRM. High booth cost. No named contacts linked.

**Bad:** “Must attend — huge awareness. Confidence 92%.”

**Good:** Skip. “Attendance is not decision-maker density. No named live contacts to meet. Impact: booth cost with no path to a paid study.”

## 2. Niche association, competitors present

**Facts:** Membership puts Standard Bio in a room with permitting authorities and two pyrolysis OEMs.

**Good:** Join (or stay) for access to authorities. Competitive presence is a reason to be in the room, not a SWOT. Hand “who we meet and which offer” to growth.

**Bad:** “Join so we can out-brand PYREG and start selling carbon credits.”

## 3. Event with live CRM contacts

**Facts:** Three sell-to prospects in the registry are listed as attending. Catalog offerings already on one open deal.

**Good:** Attend. Plan named meetings, not a booth (`event-planning-engine`). Then hand off to growth for offer kind on each meeting.

**Bad:** Invent ten more attendees “who should be there.”

## 4. Channel ask

**Bad:** “Post daily on LinkedIn for reach.”

**Good:** Rank LinkedIn only if it reaches plant owners / economic buyers and can create a conversation that growth can turn into `paid_feasibility` or `machinery`. Otherwise skip or keep on watch.
