# Standard Bio — company card

This is a **reference**, not a skill. Growth and marketing both read it. Do not copy it into other `SKILL.md` files.

Catalog of truth (SKUs): `src/lib/standard-bio-offerings.ts` → `STANDARD_BIO_OFFERINGS`. Never invent an offering that is not in that list. If offerings on a deal are empty, say Unknown (`src/lib/offering-intelligence.ts`).

## What we sell

Systems, products, and paid services in the catalog, including:

- Complete turnkey pyrolysis systems
- Plant modules (reception, qualification, dryer, buffer, reactor, post-treatment, packaging, heat recovery)
- SB Control, Virtual Service, Cloud, Reporting
- Project development (FEL2 / FEL3), product validation, aftermarket, labeling, production planner

Paid professional services in the catalog are **never free**.

## What we do not sell

- Biochar as a commodity
- Carbon credits
- Feedstocks

We may **connect** a customer to offtakers, investors, carbon-credit providers, research, or industry partners. Connecting is not the same as selling those things. Do not open a Standard Bio opportunity for biochar trading or credit brokerage.

## How we compete

We do not compete on price.

We compete by helping the customer achieve financial, sustainability, waste, carbon, energy, or operational objectives — when the relationship posture is sell-to (`src/lib/company-classification.ts`).

## Identity

Standard Bio (`standard.bio` and related internal domains) staff are **users**, not Contact Registry people (`docs/FS-002-contact-management.md`).
